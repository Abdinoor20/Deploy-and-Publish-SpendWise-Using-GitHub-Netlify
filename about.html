About SpendWise

SpendWise is a small expense tracker built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

How it works

  1. Add income or an expense on the Dashboard.
  2. See your balance and monthly spending by category.
  3. Search, filter, export or delete entries on the Transactions page.

Your data

Transactions are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing your browser data or switching devices removes or hides your records. Use Export CSV to keep a copy.

How it is published

The source lives in a public GitHub repository. Netlify rebuilds and publishes the site automatically each time a change is pushed to the main branch.
